Lost in the Hajar Mountains

Last winter, Saeed and his family went to the Hajar Mountains in Ras Al Khaimah. The weather was cool and sunny. They started walking at eight o'clock in the morning. The path was rocky, and there were tall mountains all around them.

After two hours, Saeed stopped to take a photo of a small canyon. When he turned around, his family was gone. He was lost! Saeed felt frightened, but he made a good decision. He stayed in one place and did not walk away. He drank some water from his bottle and waited.

Then he remembered his father's whistle in his bag. He blew it three times. Soon, he heard his father calling his name. Saeed was very happy. Now he always walks next to his family on adventures.

The Day the Sky Turned Orange

Omar was playing football with his friends in the park when the sky suddenly turned orange. A strong wind started to blow, and sand flew everywhere. “It's a sandstorm!” shouted his friend Khalid.

The boys couldn't see the goal anymore. Sand got into their eyes and mouths. Omar remembered his teacher's words: if there is a sandstorm, go inside quickly and cover your face. He pulled his T-shirt over his nose and led his friends to the small shop next to the park.

Inside, the shopkeeper gave them cold water. Through the window, they watched the sand hit the cars. After thirty minutes, the wind became calm and the sky was blue again.

That evening, Omar told his family about the storm. His mother smiled. “You stayed calm and helped your friends. I'm proud of you,” she said.
